02Utility bills

Columns: Month, kWh, Peak_kW (or Peak_kVA), PF, Cost. PF and Cost are optional. PDF bills are read automatically (units consumed, max demand, power factor, bill amount) and added here as rows. Leave empty to take energy figures from the building manual.
Gas bills (optional) —
Columns: Month, gas use (kWh, therms, m³/SCM, MMBtu or GJ — put the unit in the header, e.g. "SCM"), Cost. Converted to kWh. Gas savings come from the heating strategies.
Water bills (optional) —
Columns: Month, water use (kL, m³, litres, gallons or CCF — put the unit in the header), Cost. Converted to kL. Water savings come from less cooling-tower make-up when chiller load falls.
